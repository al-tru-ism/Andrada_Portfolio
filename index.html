A little more about me

I’ve always been drawn to the stories hidden inside noisy datasets. Whether it was cleaning hundreds of thousands of messy environmental sensor logs during my internship or building computer vision models to track urban foot traffic, I learned early on that the best analytical work starts with trusted ground truth. My background in IT and security gives me a healthy obsession with data validation, privacy, and making sure the underlying pipeline is airtight before drawing any conclusions.

I’m currently looking for my first full-time role as a Data Analyst. I want to join a team where I can dig into raw databases, build clean dashboards, and help translate complex operational data into straightforward answers that drive better decisions.
